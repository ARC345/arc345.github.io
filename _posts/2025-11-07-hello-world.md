# Welcome to My Corner of the Web **arc-blog**

Welcome to arc-blog — my small corner of the internet. I’m studying Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, but this space isn’t just about code. It’s about learning, experimenting, and trying to make sense of things — both in technology and in life.

I like building, thinking, and exploring. Some days that means writing algorithms; other days it means getting lost in ideas, places, or the ocean — I’m an advanced open-water diver, and I love the calm beneath the chaos.

Here, I’ll share parts of my journey — projects, reflections, lessons, and moments that feel worth remembering. No filters, no algorithms — just thoughts evolving in public.

Thanks for stopping by. Hope you find something here that resonates, or at least makes you pause for a second.
